# Iowa Freight Intelligence

## Overview

Iowa Freight Intelligence is a logistics intelligence platform focused on connecting freight carriers, equipment owners, brokers, shippers, and businesses through actionable data.

The long-term vision is to become the central marketplace for freight intelligence within Iowa by identifying opportunities, reducing empty miles, improving equipment utilization, and creating valuable connections throughout the transportation industry.

---

## Mission

Increase efficiency across Iowa's transportation network by organizing publicly available data and industry intelligence into tools that help businesses make better decisions.

---

## Current Objectives

- Research the Iowa freight market
- Identify underserved transportation needs
- Build relationships with carriers and logistics companies
- Develop a centralized freight intelligence database
- Explore marketplace opportunities for truck and equipment leasing
- Build software tools to automate freight research

---
